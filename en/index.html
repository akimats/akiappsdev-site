About

AkiAppsDev is a sole proprietorship developing practical Android apps. We focus on small, useful features — diagnostics, calculators, life logs, parenting support, healthcare, and more — wrapped in a simple UI.

All apps are built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, following the Material 3 design guidelines and complying with the latest Android privacy requirements.

Published apps are available on the Google Play Store.
